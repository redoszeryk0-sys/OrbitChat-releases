## Orbit Chat 1.3.5

### Bug fixes
- Fixed avatar-history previews for newly saved and restored backup avatars.
- Fixed the avatar zoom slider so it can reach the full range, and kept the crop consistent across views.
- Fixed custom avatars not appearing in Account settings.
- Updated avatars on already-loaded messages immediately after an avatar change or restore.
- Added typing indicators to channels and direct messages; they clear when typing stops or the connection closes.

The portable Windows x64 package includes the in-app updater.
